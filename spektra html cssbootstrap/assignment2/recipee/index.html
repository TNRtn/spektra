Recipes

Breakfast

Omelette

Eggs, milk, vegetables, salt, and pepper.

Process

  1. Whisk eggs and milk in a bowl.
  2. Heat a pan and add vegetables.
  3. Pour the egg mixture and cook until set.
  4. Fold and serve hot.
Pancakes

Flour, baking powder, milk, and egg.

Process

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, milk, and egg.
  2. Heat a pan and pour the batter.
  3. Cook until bubbles form, then flip.
  4. Serve with syrup or fruits.
Smoothie Bowl

Banana, berries, yogurt, and granola.

Process

  1. Blend banana, berries, and yogurt.
  2. Pour into a bowl and top with granola.
  3. Add additional toppings if desired.
  4. Serve chilled.
Avocado Toast

Bread, avocado, lemon, and chili flakes.

Process

  1. Toast the bread.
  2. Mash avocado and spread on toast.
  3. Sprinkle lemon juice and chili flakes.
  4. Serve immediately.

Lunch

Burger

Bun, patty, lettuce, tomato, and cheese.

Process

  1. Cook the patty on a grill or pan.
  2. Toast the bun and assemble with toppings.
  3. Add lettuce, tomato, and cheese.
  4. Serve with fries or a side salad.
Pizza

Dough, sauce, cheese, and toppings.

Process

  1. Roll out the dough and spread sauce.
  2. Add cheese and your favorite toppings.
  3. Bake in a preheated oven at 475°F for 12-15 minutes.
  4. Slice and serve hot.
Salad

Lettuce, veggies, dressing, and protein.

Process

  1. Chop lettuce and veggies.
  2. Add your choice of protein (chicken, tofu, etc.).
  3. Toss with dressing and serve chilled.
Pasta

Pasta, sauce, and cheese.

Process

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Heat sauce in a pan and mix with pasta.
  3. Top with cheese and serve hot.

Dinner

Grilled Salmon

Salmon, olive oil, garlic, and lemon.

Process

  1. Marinate salmon with olive oil, garlic, and lemon.
  2. Grill for 4-5 minutes on each side.
  3. Serve with a side of vegetables or rice.
Steak

Beef, salt, pepper, and herbs.

Process

  1. Season steak with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  2. Cook on a hot pan or grill to desired doneness.
  3. Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
Vegetable Curry

Mixed veggies, coconut milk, and spices.

Process

  1. Sauté vegetables in a pan.
  2. Add coconut milk and spices.
  3. Simmer for 15-20 minutes and serve with rice.
Chicken Alfredo

Pasta, chicken, cream, and parmesan.

Process

  1. Cook pasta and set aside.
  2. Cook chicken in a pan and add cream and parmesan.
  3. Mix with pasta and serve hot.
